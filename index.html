Zawji habibi, rou7y, 7obbi, 7yeti w weldi…

Lyoum nhar mouch kima ay nhar… lyoum nhar li toulid fih 7ob 7yety, w ana ma najemch nfawtou bla ma n9ollek 9adeh enti t3anili.

Men nhar eli d5alt li 7yeti, barcha 7ajet tbaddlou. L9it m3ak raha, aman, w 7ob ma kontch netsawer eni najem n7ess bih haka. Enti mouch ken 7obbi… enti rou7i, ra7et 9albi, w sakinty. M3ak n7ess rou7i mahbouba, methenia, w fi blasti.

Ma bech nekdheb 3lik… el distance binetna touja3ni barcha parfois. Touja3ni khater fama barcha lahdhat netmana nkoun fehom 7dhek… netmana n3ayetlek w nchouf dh7ketek 9odemi… netmana n7odhnek bil9wi w ma nkhallikch.

Ama malgré el distance… 3omry ma 7assitha b3adet 9loubna bil3ass. Kol nhar n7ess rou7i a9rab lik akther. El masafa ma tnajjemch tna9is men 7obbi lik… wala tbadel chay men ma7abti — tzidni bark netwa7chek akther… w n7ebek akther… w nestanna l nhar eli ma yab9ach fama chay yfarra9na.

Enti dima ma3aya… fi mokhi… fi 9albi… fi kol tafsila men nhari. W hedha l site 3maltou bich yb9a notre petit coin lina a7na — nhottou fih souvenirtna, dh7ketna, klemna, w kol lahdha 7lowa binetna… bch hata ken fama kilometret binetna taw… nab9aw dima 9rib l baadhna.

Merci khaterk fi 7yeti… khaterk tkhallini n7ess bel 7ob, bel aman, w bel douceur eli dima tmanitha. Joyeux anniversaire ya rou7i… ya 7ob 9albi… ya a8la insan fi denya.

N7ebek barcha… w kol nhar n7ebek akther men nhar eli 9ablou. 🤍
